Questions

What is Tetsubi?

Tetsubi is a music player app for iPhone and Mac that plays music from a Jellyfin server. It's a Jellyfin music client: it streams your own music library from your server, and downloads albums to your device so you can listen offline.

Where does the name come from?

The name comes from the Japanese saying 徹頭徹尾 (hiragana: てっとうてつび; Hepburn: tettō tetsubi), which means "from beginning to end". We chose it because that's how Tetsubi is meant to be listened to: albums played all the way through, from the first track to the last.

Which music servers does Tetsubi work with?

Jellyfin. Tetsubi is made for Jellyfin servers and signs in with Jellyfin's Quick Connect or a Jellyfin username and password. It doesn't connect to Plex, Emby, Navidrome or Subsonic servers.

Is Tetsubi like Plexamp, but for Jellyfin?

In spirit, yes. Plexamp is Plex's music app; Tetsubi brings the same album-first feel to Jellyfin, with blurred album-art backgrounds, gapless playback and a Now Playing screen built around the cover. Tetsubi isn't made by or connected with Plex.

Can I listen to my Jellyfin music offline on iPhone?

Yes. Download any album in Tetsubi and it plays with no connection, including on the lock screen with its artwork. Downloads can keep their original quality (such as FLAC) or be converted to ALAC, AAC, MP3, Opus or WAV.

Does Tetsubi support CarPlay, AirPlay and Siri?

Yes, all three.

Is there an Android version?

Yes, it's in the works and coming soon. Tetsubi is available for iPhone and Mac first.

Does Tetsubi collect my data?

No. There's no Tetsubi account, no analytics and no adverts. The app talks only to your own Jellyfin server, plus MusicBrainz, Wikidata and Wikipedia for artist information. See the privacy policy.
